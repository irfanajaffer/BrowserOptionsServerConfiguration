Why the full product claim remains inconclusive:
- The banner proves the value supplied to ConfigureBrowser for each request, not
  the merged value read back from browser startup.
- SDK 11.0.100-preview.7.26381.103 does not expose the documented
  BrowserOptions.GetBrowserOptions(HttpContext) overload, so effective options
  could not be read back with that API.
- The Trace visitor emitted the same Information connection messages as the
  Information visitor. Distinct effective browser logging behavior was not
  observable.
- The captures came from two concurrently open pages, but do not independently
  prove separate browser profiles or InPrivate storage contexts.
- HAR files were not captured and must not be inferred from the console records.

assertion. They are not sufficient to claim that each browser effectively
